# AXIS CRM Platform Documentation

AXIS is a comprehensive CRM (Customer Relationship Management) platform designed to manage the entire sales lifecycle, from lead generation to payment collection and support.

## Core Modules & Sidebar Navigation

The platform is organized into several key modules, accessible via the sidebar:

### 1. Dashboard
- **Purpose**: Provides a high-level overview of sales performance and key metrics.
- **Key Metrics**:
  - Pipeline Value (Total value of all active deals)
  - Active Deals (Count of deals currently in progress)
  - Win Rate (Percentage of deals successfully closed)
  - New Leads (Count of recently added prospects)
- **Features**: Quick access to common actions and summaries of recent deals and upcoming activities.

### 2. Leads Management
- **All Leads**: Central repository for all sales prospects.
- **Sales Pipeline**: Visual representation of the lead journey.
- **Pipeline Kanban**: Drag-and-drop interface for managing leads through different stages.
- **Lead Allocations**: Tools for assigning leads to specific agents.
- **Bulk Tools**: Support for bulk CSV uploads and mass allocations.
- **Key Fields**: First Name, Last Name, Company, Email, Phone, Title, Lead Source, Status (New, Contacted, Qualified, etc.), Lead Score.

### 3. Interaction Activities
- **Activity Calendar**: Monthly view of scheduled interactions.
- **Tasks**: To-do list for sales agents with priorities and due dates.
- **Reminders**: Time-based notifications for follow-ups.
- **Meetings**: Scheduling and tracking of face-to-face or virtual meetings.
- **Communication Logs**: Tracking of Calls, SMS, and Emails.
- **Documents**: Centralized storage for lead/account-related files.

### 4. Accounts & Contacts
- **Accounts**: Profiles for companies and organizations.
  - **Fields**: Account Name, Industry, Website, Phone, Billing/Shipping Address, Employees, Annual Revenue.
- **Contacts**: Profiles for individual people associated with accounts.
  - **Fields**: Name, Email, Phone, Mobile, Title, Department, Reports To.
- **Deals**: History of business opportunities linked to the account/contact.

### 5. Deals & Commissions
- **All Deals**: Comprehensive list of all business opportunities.
  - **Fields**: Deal Name, Amount, Closing Date, Pipeline Stage, Probability, Deal Type.
- **Commission Requests**: Management of agent commissions linked to closed deals.
- **Commission Settings**: Configuration of commission rates and rules.

### 6. Products & Offers
- **Product List**: Catalog of products and services offered.
  - **Fields**: Product Name, Code, Unit Price, Category, Availability.
- **Offers & Promotions**: Management of discounts, seasonal offers, and special pricing.

### 7. Payments
- **Payment Records**: Tracking of all financial transactions from customers.
  - **Fields**: Receipt Number, Amount, Date, Payment Method, Transaction Reference.

### 8. Support (Case Management)
- **Cases**: Tracking of customer support issues and inquiries.
  - **Fields**: Case Number, Subject, Origin (Email, Phone, Web), Status, Priority, Description.
- **Case Types**: Customizable categories for different types of support requests.

### 9. Reports & Analytics
- **Sales Reports**: Insights into revenue trends and target achievement.
- **Agent Performance**: Metrics on individual agent activity and success.
- **Pipeline Analytics**: Analysis of conversion rates and stage duration.
- **Payment Reports**: Summary of collections and pending payments.

### 10. Configuration & Settings
- **Entity Types**: Define custom types for Accounts, Deals, Meetings, etc.
- **Lookups**: Manage Interest Levels, Lead Sources, and Cancellation Reasons.
- **System Settings**: Global platform configurations, including sales pipelines and product categories.

## Platform Features

- **Multi-Tenant Support**: Each organization operates in its own secure environment.
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**: Granular permissions for different user roles (Admin, Agent, Supervisor).
- **Proactive Notifications**: Integrated reminders and alerts for critical tasks.
- **Centralized Authentication**: Secure login proxied through a central identity provider.
- **Audit Logging**: Comprehensive tracking of all entity changes for accountability.
